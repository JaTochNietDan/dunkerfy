# Dunkerfy sound bank

29 original synthesized effects were created for Dunkerfy. Their audio and the reproducible `scripts/generate-sounds.mjs` generator are distributed under the repository's MIT license. Names describe the effects; none are sampled from commercial games, TV shows, or meme videos.

## Wilhelm scream

Source: USC Cinema / Sunset Editorial sound-effects collection, digitized and provided by Craig Smith. Performer attributed by Wikimedia Commons as likely Sheb Wooley (1951).

- Collection: https://archive.org/details/SSE_Library_VOICES
- Original file: `SCREAM/VOXScrm_Man eaten by alligator; screams [Wilhelm_CS_USC.wav`
- Corroborating description and excerpt: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wilhelm_Scream.ogg
- License indicated by both sources: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Retrieved 2026-10-15. Converted to mono 44.1 kHz / 16-bit PCM using macOS afconvert, excerpted from 27.00–28.65 seconds, peak normalized with short fades. The source recording is not committed in full.

All bundled WAV files are peak limited to 0.70 full scale, with short edge fades. Duration and category metadata are in `catalog.json`. macOS system sounds remain on the user's system and are not redistributed.

Source WAV SHA-256: `7556ab21a83cdb92628aad4bcec7f6951a7258614e49458df880b87a14ae7b13`. Bundled Wilhelm excerpt SHA-256: `022548cd8f647bd50df022cb5ad2a7e11a0371d59458d2fc5f1391ac3541ef85`.
